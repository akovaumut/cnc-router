# CNC Router
## Overview
  I decided to design and build my own CNC router to satisfy my growing curiosity about CNC machines and deepen my understanding of the engineering concepts involved in the design and construction process.

## Technical Specifications
 - **Working Area:** 480x480x70mm
 - 0.75kW 24000RPM Spindle with ER16 collet
 - 3 axis controlled by 4(2 for Y) NEMA 17 17HS4401 step motors & A4988 motor drivers
 - CNC Shield v3(for Arduino UNO) used for movement control & Arduino UNO with GRBL installed

## Design Objectives
 - Minimize manufacturing costs without compromising essential machine functionality
 - Enable machining of 6061 aluminum plates and sheets

## Current Status
 - Mechanical assembly completed; machine calibration and improvements ongoing
 - Working on fixture plate
 - (Last updated: October 15, 2026

## Known Limitations
 - Unintended axial displacement of the X-axis ballscrew
